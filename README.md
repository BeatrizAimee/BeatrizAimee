<h1 align="center">Hi, I’m Beatriz Aimée</h1>

<p align="center">
  Integrated circuits, digital hardware and low-level software.

I am interested in analog and mixed-signal IC design, physical layout, EDA and
machine-learning-assisted circuit design. I also enjoy working from RTL/FPGA systems and
computer architecture down to embedded software.

## Featured projects

- [C TDD Light Scheduler](https://github.com/BeatrizAimee/c-tdd-light-scheduler) —
  C99, Unity, CMock and saved LCOV coverage.
- [STM32 Bare-Metal LED Matrix](https://github.com/BeatrizAimee/stm32-bare-metal-led-matrix) —
  startup code, interrupts and drivers for an RGB LED matrix.
- [FPGA Video Controller](https://github.com/BeatrizAimee/Video-controller) —
  SystemVerilog work around framebuffer display, SDRAM and clock-domain crossing.
